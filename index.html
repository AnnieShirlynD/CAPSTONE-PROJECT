Cyber Threat Prevention

Actions to take when a URL looks suspicious.

🚫Block risky destinations

Do not open a URL flagged as suspicious. This demo warns users but does not block websites at browser or network level.

🔐Protect credentials

Never enter passwords, OTPs, recovery codes or banking details after following an unexpected message link.

🛡Use verified security services

Check suspicious URLs with your organization's security team or an approved threat-intelligence service.

📣Report phishing

Report suspicious emails, messages and websites to the relevant service provider or security administrator.

Threat Intelligence Library

Understand common online threats.

Phishing

Fraudulent pages and messages designed to steal login details or financial information. Verify the destination independently.

Typosquatting

Domains that imitate familiar brands using misspellings or deceptive characters. Check the exact domain name.

Malicious redirects

Links that send visitors to unexpected destinations. Avoid unknown short links and inspect destinations with trusted tools.

Credential theft

Fake login pages that capture passwords. Use a password manager and multifactor authentication.

Malware distribution

Websites that encourage harmful downloads. Keep software updated and use reputable endpoint protection.
